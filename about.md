# About Me

Hi, I’m SeoHyun-Han, a computer science student interested in software development and various areas of technology.

I’m still learning, and most of what I know has come from actually building things, running into problems, and figuring out how to solve them. I enjoy understanding not only how something works, but also why it works that way.

I’m currently exploring web development, backend development, game development, and other areas of software engineering.

# About This Blog

This blog is a place where I document what I learn through hands-on development.

Rather than writing only about finished results, I want to record the process behind them — the problems I encounter, the approaches I try, the mistakes I make, and the solutions I discover.

Some posts may be simple notes about a technology I learned, while others may cover a problem that took hours to understand or fix.

I hope these records can serve as a reference for my future self, and perhaps be useful to someone who encounters a similar problem.

# Current Projects

Game Development
